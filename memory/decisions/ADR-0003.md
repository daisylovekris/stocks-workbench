# ADR-0003: Fail-closed source identity

## Scope

`scope: project`

## Date

`date: 2026-07-29`

## Context

Path-only or summary-only evidence can silently drift and cannot establish the bytes reviewed.

## Decision

Every formal evidence claim binds repository-relative path, commit snapshot, raw SHA-256, and role.

## Alternatives

Trust current worktree paths; trust summaries; require commit-bound identity.

## Consequences

Missing, malformed, or mismatched identity blocks the claim and requires adjudication or evidence refresh.

## Authority boundaries

Identity proves bytes at a snapshot only; it does not infer model identity, correctness, permission, or current business truth.
