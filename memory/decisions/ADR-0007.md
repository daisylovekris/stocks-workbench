# ADR-0007: Routing and independent review separation

`decision_basis: pending_owner_decision`

## Scope

`scope: project`

## Date

`date: 2026-07-29`

## Context

A routing label states intended task allocation, while a benchmark claim requires a bound observed run.

## Decision

Routing policy records and observed independent-review runs remain different record types.

## Alternatives

Rank models from routing labels; merge routing and review records; retain typed separation.

## Consequences

Luna, Terra, and Sol stay routing-only until bound runs exist, and one run never becomes a general ranking.

## Authority boundaries

Routing evidence cannot prove quality, cost, latency, or review outcome.
