# ADR-0009: SWP candidate-only output

## Status

- `ID`: `ADR-0009`
- `title`: `SWP candidate-only output`
- `status`: `accepted`

## Decision basis

`decision_basis: documents_existing_authority`

## Scope

`scope: SWP v0.1`

## Date

`date: 2026-07-29`

## Context

The frozen design and closure explicitly separate candidate preparation from approval and downstream action.

## Decision

SWP v0.1 machine authority ends at a candidate ready for human review.

## Alternatives

Permit autonomous final output; stop at diagnostics; preserve candidate-only completion.

## Consequences

A successful run can establish candidate readiness but cannot establish approval or execution authority.

## Authority boundaries

No trading action, Git action, final review verdict, or automatic trigger is authorized.
